Free to use. Paid to go unsigned.

Lutrin costs nothing to run and never will: every layout, every chart, every kit and both export formats work without a licence. A deck compiled without one carries a discreet Made with Lutrin at the bottom right of each slide. A licence removes that line. There is nothing else on the other side of the payment — no slide cap, no export limit, no feature held back.

A seat is a person

Not a machine. Install Lutrin on your laptop, your desktop and the build server: they all run on the same key. The people count is declarative — nothing in the tool counts your colleagues.

Activate once, then compile offline

Reading the licence is a read of one local file. A licence keeps applying for 30 days without reaching the network, and a build never waits on anything.

Nothing is retroactive

The attribution is written into the file at compile time. A deck you exported while licensed stays clean forever, whatever happens to the licence afterwards.
