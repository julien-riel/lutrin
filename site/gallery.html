one deck · eight brands

The same slide, in eight kits.

Every tile below is the same file — specimen.deck.md, the same words in the same order — compiled by the engine at HEAD into a different kit. Nothing here is a screenshot: each one is the real compiled deck in a frame, so what you are comparing is what the compiler actually produces. The words never move; everything that does comes from the kit.

Install any of them and your next build comes out in it. The commands assume lutrin on your PATH — npx lutrin … does the same thing. Each tile is the fourth slide of the deck, the one where the whole palette is on screen at once; click it for the other three.

what you just installed

A kit is data. Never code.

Free, on every tier

Installing a kit checks no licence, and building with one costs nothing. Kits are not the paid part of Lutrin — the only thing a licence changes is the attribution line.

An archive, not a script

A .deckkit carries a manifest, design tokens, and optionally layouts, fonts and logos. kit install refuses any executable entry, which is what makes installing one from a URL defensible.

These eight are themes only

They vary colour, type and geometry, because that is the axis you can compare eight of at a glance. A kit can also ship layouts of its own — kit-slate does.

None of these eight is a real organisation. They are archetypes — a newsroom, a university, a supplier — invented so the page can show you a brand without borrowing anyone's. Yours is the one worth building: the kit editor puts every colour, font and layout beside a deck the engine just compiled, and contact@lutrin.app is where the conversation starts if you would rather hand over the brand guidelines and get the kit back.
