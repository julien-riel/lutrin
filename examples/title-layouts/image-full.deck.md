# The scrim, and what it costs

<!-- layout: split -->

- The photograph fills the page and a veil of the cover surface lies over it,
  so the title has a ground to be read against
- It is `chrome.cover.scrimAlpha`, and it sits at **0.6** — weaker than the
  0.85 a section divider uses, where the photograph only tints a band of brand
  colour and the picture is not the point
- The cost is real: the cover ink had its contrast checked against the cover
  background and against nothing else, so a dark or busy photograph can take
  the pair under the threshold with nothing to say so

The engine measures colours, never pixels. Raise the token in the kit, or
choose the photograph for the ink.
