# The composition

<!-- layout: split -->

- Logo at the top left, on the page margin
- The accent bar, then the title, then the subtitle
- The byline on the last line of the page
- Everything across the full width, margin to margin

Nothing here changed when `titleLayout:` landed, and that is the point: a deck
that names no layout must come out exactly as it did before.
