# What the layout decides

<!-- layout: split -->

- The photograph runs to the top, the bottom and the right edge. It is a half
  of the page, not a picture set into a margin
- The text keeps the left half, minus the page margin on both of its sides
- The logo, the accent bar, the title, the subtitle and the byline all sit on
  that column: they move together or the composition breaks

The credit rides in the byline, which is where a real title slide puts it —
CC BY-SA asks that the attribution travel with the photograph.
