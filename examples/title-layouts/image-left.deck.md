# Why the logo moves

<!-- layout: split -->

- On this layout the page margin at the top left is UNDER the photograph
- A signature left there would sit on the image, which is the one arrangement
  no kit could correct from its own side
- So the logo and the accent bar travel with the text column, and the cover
  reads as two halves rather than as a photograph with something on it

The proportion is `chrome.cover.splitRatio`, a theme token: a kit that wants
a third rather than a half sets it once, for every deck it dresses.
